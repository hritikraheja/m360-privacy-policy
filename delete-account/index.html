Delete Your Account & Data

Muslim 360

Last updated: 26th August 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Muslim 360 account and the data associated with it. Anyone can read this page — no sign-in and no app installation is required to view these instructions or to submit a deletion request.

1. How to Request Deletion

Send an email to the address below and we will delete your account and the data associated with it. This is a deletion request address, not a general support queue — an email sent here starts the deletion process.

Email: sahil.createx@gmail.com

Email a deletion request

Steps

  1. Email sahil.createx@gmail.com with the subject line Delete My Muslim 360 Account.
  2. In the body of the email, include the email address or phone number registered with your Muslim 360 account, and your display name if you set one. That is the only information we need in order to locate your account.
  3. State that you are requesting permanent deletion of your account and data.
  4. We reply to confirm that we received the request. We may ask you to confirm from the registered email address, or to sign in once, purely to verify that the account belongs to you. No sign-in is ever required to read this page or to send the request itself.
  5. Once verified, we delete the account and data described below and email you a confirmation when it is complete.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Once your account is deleted, your saved settings and history are gone and cannot be restored. You are welcome to create a new account afterwards, but it will start empty.

2. What Gets Deleted

When we process your request, the following is permanently deleted from our systems:

  • Your account — the account record itself and your login credentials, so the account can no longer be signed into or recovered.
  • Profile information — your name or display name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and any other profile details you provided.
  • App preferences and settings — your saved city or location preference, prayer time calculation method, juristic (madhab) setting, notification and adhan preferences, language, and theme.

3. How Long It Takes

Stage Timeframe
We acknowledge your request by email Within 7 days of receiving it
Account and associated data deleted from active systems Within 30 days of verifying the request

If anything delays a request beyond 30 days, we will email you to explain why and give you a revised date. Backup copies are not accessible for ordinary use and are never restored into service after a deletion has been completed.

4. Data We Are Required to Retain

A small amount of data may outlive your account, but only where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect the service. We keep the minimum necessary, and none of it is used for analytics, marketing, or personalisation.

What we retain Why How long
Transaction, purchase, and invoice records — only if you ever made a paid purchase or held a subscription Required by tax and accounting law Up to 8 years from the end of the relevant financial year
A record that a deletion request was made and completed: the request date, completion date, and the identifier used to locate the account Evidence of compliance with data protection obligations Up to 3 years
Records relating to fraud, abuse, a security incident, or an active legal claim Security, and legal obligations to preserve evidence Only for as long as the matter requires, then deleted
Aggregated and anonymised statistics, for example the total number of users in a city Understanding overall app usage Retained indefinitely — this data holds no personal identifiers and cannot be linked back to you

If none of the above applies to you — for example, if you never made a purchase — then nothing personal about you is retained beyond the deletion request record.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Some data, such as cached prayer times and locally saved preferences, lives on your phone rather than on our servers. Deleting your account does not erase what is stored on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app, or open Settings → Apps → Muslim 360 → Storage → Clear data on your device.

6. Questions

If you have any question about deletion, about the status of a request you have already sent, or about what data we hold on you, contact us at sahil.createx@gmail.com. Every deletion request sent to this address is honoured.
